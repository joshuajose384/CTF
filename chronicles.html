THE CHRONICLES OF DOOM

OFFICIAL HISTORY OF THE REIGN

  1. The Orphan. Victor von Doom is born to the Zefiro people of Latveria, and swears vengeance upon a cruel world.
  2. The Scholar. He masters both sorcery and science, rivalled only by Reed Richards, whom he will never forgive.
  3. The Ascension. In the year 1962, Victor von Doom overthrows King Vladimir and seizes the throne of Latveria. From that day the nation answers to one man alone.
  4. The Mourning. Doom's one true love was lost to his earliest experiment. He guards her name above all things; even his enemies dare not speak it.
  5. The Doomsday. With reality fracturing, the Sovereign prepares a final decree, sealed within the throne room for his eyes only.
